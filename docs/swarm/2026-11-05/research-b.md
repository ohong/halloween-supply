## Starlight Witch

The selected hat, celestial cape, and pendant keep new purchases below $100.
The dress and footwear are costly to replace and work as owned basics.
The cape is sheer black with silver detail. It differs from the illustrated embroidered navy velvet.

Two approaches were compared:

1. Buy a finished celestial cape and wear an existing dress. This needs no sewing but uses three merchants.
2. Decorate an owned plain cape with fabric stars. This may cost less, but requires an owned cape, material sourcing, and sewing.

The first approach gives a concrete existing-catalog purchase list with a ten-minute dressing estimate.

## Autumn Scarecrow

The hat and cotton fringe carry the costume's visual cues.
Existing overalls, a plaid shirt, and scraps provide the rest.
This kit requires owned overalls; it is unsuitable when the shopper lacks them.

Two approaches were compared:

1. Buy the hat and a full yard of Straw cotton; reuse patch scraps. This costs $24.99 and uses two merchants.
2. Buy cheaper muslin and a separate patch bundle. This adds a merchant and may create quantity or order-minimum problems.

The $2.50 Bolt & Spool catalog unit is a quarter yard, not a full yard.
Its [public page](https://boltandspool.com/products/cotton-muslin-natural-1) displays $10 per yard, but the quarter-yard quantity could not be rechecked.
The [Zelouf muslin collection](https://zelouffabrics.com/collections/muslin-fabric) states a $20 order minimum, despite a $4.99 fabric price.
Neither cheaper option is the selected purchase.

The selected full yard gives more fitting margin and a closer straw color.
The sixty-minute sewing estimate excludes fabric washing, drying, and learning stitches.

## Midnight Pirate

The tricorn and burgundy sash establish the pirate cues for $29.
The owned shirt, coat, and boots use simpler fabric and trim than the reference.
The owned long coat is an explicit prerequisite.

Two approaches were compared:

1. Buy the hat and sash; wear owned plain layers. This avoids new apparel sizing and costs $29.
2. Buy period garments from the catalog. The $12 shirt is Small only; the $56.75 coat has unknown stock.

The first approach keeps the kit usable without implying broad availability for the cheap shirt.
Buying an in-stock brocade-style coat starts at $150 before other purchases.
That more elaborate look does not meet the $100 new-pieces target.
