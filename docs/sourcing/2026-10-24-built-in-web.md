# Costume sourcing handoff — 24 October 2026

## Scope and evidence

Built-in web search and page retrieval only. No external sourcing, scraping, merchant, or model APIs were used. This is a separate research handoff for the ongoing build. It does not change catalog snapshots, product imagery, graph data, or the deployed site.

The existing catalog already held 75 candidates: three for each of 25 pieces. I attempted canonical product-page retrieval for all 75. Of those first-pass URLs, 49 returned readable pages and 26 did not. Follow-up searches recovered some alternate URLs and identified additional candidates. A failed retrieval is not proof that the product is gone.

The table below supplies one proposed pick for each piece. The JSON companion retains all 75 earlier leads with their prior catalog price and first-pass page retrieval outcome. Prior prices are explicitly historical, not reconfirmed facts.

These are sourcing candidates, not 25 checkout-ready guarantees. Web results can be cached, and stock varies by size. No purchases or delivery checks for a specific address were made.

## Status labels

- **Page reports stock:** retrieved merchant content states stock for the product or a specified variant. Other sizes remain unverified.
- **Check variant:** price and product are sourced, but selected size/color stock needs checking.
- **Too late for Halloween:** the retrieved listing indicates a lead time beyond the event.

Generic Shopify sold-out, cart, and notification markup can coexist with purchase controls. I did not interpret that alone as proof that every variant is unavailable.

## Integration boundary

This research is intentionally not imported into the checkout-ready `CostumeSnapshot` format. That format requires confirmed product facts and exactly three matches per piece. Unknown variant availability and destination-dependent prices should remain explicit until the main build verifies them.

For the main build: select variants, reconcile currency and stock, confirm dispatch/delivery, review replacement images, and then update the catalog through its existing workflow. Preserve the remaining candidate leads as alternatives, not as automatically verified inventory.
