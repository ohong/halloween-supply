# Catalog expansion D

Researched October 26, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Exact UTC check times, selected variants, prices, image sources, and evidence URLs are in `expansion-d.json`.

Five costumes add distinct silhouettes: an antique diving bell, armored dragon rider, firebird wings, an ivory ice ruler, and a horned woodland faun. Each has four or five charged components. Each component has three distinct USD prices. Merchants’ shipping charges, taxes, and arrival dates are not confirmed.

New accessories use built-in web discovery and direct primary merchant Shopify feeds. The selected variants report `available: true`. The cached WebP images contain the actual merchant photographs. Generated costume images are styling references rather than product photographs. No Firecrawl, database publication, deployment, checkout, or purchase occurs in this research step.
