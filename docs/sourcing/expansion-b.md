# Catalog expansion B

Five additions use a different defining silhouette or character cue: Art Deco Robot, Haunted Prom Queen, Headless Horseman, Phantom of the Opera, and Silent Film Star.

All five snapshots pass `validateSnapshot()` with required in-stock defaults, three distinct USD prices per component, and no blocked merchants. They contain 21 component rows and 63 product matches. New defining product evidence and editorial records are in `expansion-b.json`.

Research used built-in web search and primary merchant pages. Direct public merchant variant feeds confirmed exact prices, stock and photographs. Several search excerpts had old prices or stock. Current feeds took precedence. No Firecrawl, database writes, deployment or purchase occurred in this batch.

Existing reviewed base garments keep their original product IDs, source evidence, photographs and check dates. This batch does not claim those base listings received a new stock check. Checkout must recheck current merchant facts.

## Defining pieces and honest compromises

- Robot: Pure Costumes Alchemist face mask. Silver is default; copper and gold repeat the same sculpt at different prices. Charcoal waistcoat, silver trousers and gloves form a wearable metallic silhouette. Shopper supplies a black shirt and comfortable shoes.
- Haunted prom queen: white Prom Queen sash, burgundy velvet gown, rose crown and dark boots. Two plain sashes and one printed horror sash offer three prices. Makeup and a faded corsage can be added from home. The Cappel's product photograph is only 135 by 240 source pixels; the cached display copy is enlarged from that exact photograph. No extra detail was generated.
- Headless horseman: California Costumes 00742 illusion bundle from Pure Costumes, Abracadabra NYC and Toynk. Each offer includes the upper garments and illusion supports. Pants and boots are separate rows. The same bundle has different prices and available sizes across merchants; Toynk currently offers Medium. Mesh visibility is limited. No separate cape is charged.
- Phantom: three white one-sided masks, black cape, ruffled shirt, waistcoat and boots. Shopper supplies black trousers. The cape reuses its original burgundy-lining product facts.
- Silent film star: black top hat, ivory shirt, charcoal waistcoat, black high-waist trousers and white stage makeup. The makeup options are **three jar volumes of the same formula**, not different colors or a kit. The smallest available jar is sufficient for a single costume. Applicators, setting powder and black details are separate supplies; comfortable black shoes and a handmade dialogue card come from home.

## Evidence and limits

New defining offers use actual merchant photographs cached in `public/products/price-tiers/`. Product-level source URLs, exact selected variants, prices and stock evidence appear in the JSON. Generated costume reference images are handled separately by the image team and show styling inspiration.
