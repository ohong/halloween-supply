# Publication checks for the 50-costume catalog

The expansion adds 25 looks to the original 25. Each new component has three
merchant choices with distinct USD prices. Generated costume images are style
references; merchant photographs show the actual products.

Before publication on October 26, 2026, the live database contained newer routine
merchant checks than the bundled original catalogs. The new records carry forward
the shared products' price, availability, sizes, and check timestamps. Original
research evidence remains attached. This updates 226 shared-product placements;
it does not replace or roll back the original 25 database records.

Two reused choices had become sold out:

- Mad Tea Hatter now uses the available [New Colonies White Ruffle Shirt](https://www.costumesandcollectibles.com/products/new-colonies-white-ruffle-shirt?variant=21374030905403)
  in place of the Bard shirt. The white polyester shirt is $69.95 at the recorded
  check and lists Small through X-Large.
- Phoenix Rising replaces sold-out crumple cotton with [Orange Acrylic Felt](https://britexfabrics.com/products/orange-acrylic-felt?variant=47850957275456).
  The recorded $12.99 buys one yard of 72-inch-wide felt for DIY flame cuffs.
  Thread, scissors, and wrist bands remain shopper-supplied.

Some refreshed boot alternatives have unknown stock. Their stock remains unknown;
each new costume's default selections are recorded as in stock. Checkout still
checks merchant facts again. Arrival by Halloween is not guaranteed, especially
for made-to-order garments. Several DIY builds need the extra supplies listed
in their component notes and editorial tips.

Runtime verification compares database-backed pages to the database's current
facts. It allows only price, stock, sizes, and check timestamps to change during
routine refresh. Product identity, source evidence, merchant photographs,
component choices, and other catalog facts must still match the reviewed files.
